Detailed Scanning and Inventory Management Guide

What is the main Purpose ?

The Inventory Management System on Kasi Ledger allows you to Utilize your smartphone's/PC's barcode scanner for easy stock management. Our user-friendly interface allows you to track incoming and outgoing items, ensuring real-time inventory data is always accessible. Stay informed about stock levels to avoid shortages and enhance profitability. All you do is to upload your products, this helps you to know how much inventory you have without the need of manual stock counting

Since the system uses the WEB, it allows you to use any device whether it's a Laptop or your Personal Smart Phone, allowing you to have access to you Business Information at anytime.

Below is a step to step guide on how to use this system

Accessing the Inventory Page

  1. Navigate from home page to inventory section kasiledger/inventory
  2. Enter admin credentials when prompted
  3. System validates authentication
  4. Access granted to inventory interface

Security and Access Control For the Inventory Page

Admin Authentication

One of the issues we aim to address is theft, which is why we have secured the inventory page with an admin password. This password is set by a trusted person within your organization. In most cases, if untrusted individuals gain access to the inventory page, they could enter incorrect product details, potentially leading to theft, especially within the organization. Managers may choose to share the general login password with employees to grant access to various pages, but the admin password should remain protected, based on management's decision, to prevent theft-related issue.

  • Access restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Admin password required for inventory page access
  • Security measures prevent unauthorized modifications
  • Helps maintain accurate inventory records
  • Reduces risk of internal theft

Camera Setup and Scanning

Scanner Setup

Once You have accessed the inventory page, you can begin to scan in inventory starting with Setting up Your Camera, Your Camera set up is done on your web browser(Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and more).

  • Browser will request camera permissions
  • Grant necessary permissions when prompted
  • Ensure adequate lighting in scanning area

Troubleshooting Scanner Issues

  • Check browser camera permissions
  • Verify device camera functionality
  • Refresh page after granting permissions
  • Try alternative browser if issues persist

Product Details Entry

Required Product Information

Once The camera is set up, you are ready to scan in products, where every field is required.

  • Product Name (unique identifier)
  • Price Per Unit (selling price)
  • Cost Per Unit (purchase cost)
  • Initial Quantity (Single or Bulk)
  • Product Category
  • Product Barcode Scanned by the Camera

Product Category

Setting Up Categories

  1. Access category management section
  2. Click "Add Category"
  3. Enter category details
  4. Save new category

Scanning Process

  1. Complete all product details first
  2. Open scanner in browser
  3. Position barcode 4-6 inches from camera
  4. Wait for confirmation beep sound
  5. Verify scanned product in table
Important: Products can only be deleted during initial scanning out phase.

Search and Filter Options

Detailed Scanning and Point Of Sale Guide

What is the main Purpose ?

The Point Of Sale system works hand in hand with the inventory management, it's function is to process sales to daily customers, by scanning the products out which significantly reduces overall quantity from your inventory.

The system facilitates sales transactions in retail or service environments. It enables your business to process sales quickly, and manage customer interactions seamlessly, all while integrating with your inventory and financial systems for real-time updates


Prerequisites

Ensure all products are already registered in the Inventory Management System before proceeding with sales.

Processing a Sale

1. Scanner Setup

Enable your browser camera to scan product barcodes.

2. Product Scanning

Scan the product barcode to automatically populate product details in the sales table. The information will match exactly what was uploaded in the Inventory Management System.

Important: If a product is:

  • Not in inventory
  • Marked as out of stock

You will receive a "Product Not Found" message.

3. Completing the Sale

  1. Enter the customer's email address for receipt delivery
  2. Click "Process Sale" button
  3. Wait for processing (this takes a few seconds)

4. Receipt Handling

After processing:

  • You'll see an option to save the receipt
  • The system automatically sends the receipt to the customer's email

A confirmation message will appear: "Receipt sent to [customer's email]"

Detailed Scanning and Income statement Guide

What is the main Purpose ?

An Income Statement, also known as a Profit and Loss Statement, is a financial document that summarizes a company's revenues, costs, and expenses during a specific period. It shows how revenue is transformed into net income, providing a clear picture of a company's profitability.


Tools to generate key financial reports, such as Income Statements, which show your business's profitability by summarizing revenues, costs, and expenses over a specific period. This helps you assess performance, track growth, and make informed financial decisions

Components of the Income Statement

Automated Fields

These fields are automatically calculated based on monthly transactions:

  • Sales: Total revenue from all sales transactions for the month. This is automatically calculated from your sales records.
  • Cost of Sales: Total cost of goods sold for the month. This is automatically pulled from your purchase and inventory records.

Operating Expenses

These expenses need to be manually entered:

  • Rent: Monthly rental payments for business premises
  • Utilities: Electricity, water, gas, internet, etc.
  • Salaries: Employee wages and related costs
  • Marketing: Advertising and promotional expenses
  • Insurance: Business insurance premiums

Other Financial Items

  • Other Income: Revenue from sources other than main business activities
  • Other Expenses: Miscellaneous expenses not covered in operating expenses
  • Interest: Interest payments on loans or debt
  • Tax: Business tax expenses
  • Dividends: Profit distributed to shareholders

System Fields

  • Profile: Links the income statement to a specific business profile
  • Created At: Timestamp when the record was created
  • Updated At: Timestamp of the last update to the record

Important Notes

  • All monetary fields can handle values up to 12 digits with 2 decimal places
  • Sales and Cost of Sales are automatically calculated monthly - no manual input needed
  • Regular updates to operating expenses ensure accurate financial reporting
  • The system maintains an audit trail through created_at and updated_at timestamps
