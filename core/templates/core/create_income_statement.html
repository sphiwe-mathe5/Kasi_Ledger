How to Use This Form

  • Sales: Enter the total revenue from your primary business activities.
  • Cost of sales: Input the direct costs associated with producing your goods or services.
  • Rent, Utilities, Salaries, Marketing, Insurance: Enter your operating expenses for each category.
  • Other income: Include any additional revenue not part of your primary business activities.
  • Other expenses: Add any additional expenses not covered in the other categories.
  • Interest: Enter any interest paid on loans or other debts.
  • Tax: Input the amount of income tax you expect to pay.
  • Dividends: If applicable, enter any dividends paid to shareholders.
  • All fields are required. If you don't have a value for a particular field, leave it as 0.
  • Use decimal points for cents (e.g., 1000.50 for R 1,000.50).
  • After filling out all fields, click "Create Income Statement" to generate your statement. Read Detailed Guide
