Welcome To Kasi Ledger

Kasi Ledger is the all-in-one business management tool built for township and small businesses. We know the challenges you face — from tracking stock, recording sales, and managing employees, to keeping up with finances and planning for growth.

That’s why we created Kasi Ledger: a simple, affordable, and powerful solution that combines inventory tracking, sales & receipts, financial statements, and AI-powered insights into one easy-to-use platform.

Run your business smarter, save time, and focus on growth with Kasi Ledger.

Real Time
Inventory Tracking

Utilize your smartphone's barcode scanner for easy stock management. Our user-friendly interface allows you to track incoming and outgoing items, ensuring real-time inventory data is always accessible. Stay informed about stock levels to avoid shortages and enhance profitability.

Financial Statements

Kasi Ledger calculates profits as items are sold, providing instant insights into daily earnings. It also generates financial statements, including income statements and balance sheets, giving you a view of your business's financial health. Monitor costs and sales in real-time to make informed decisions.
