Scanning Guide

How to Scan Products

  1. Choose "Scan In" for new inventory or "Scan Out" for sales.
  2. Position the product's barcode in front of your device's camera.
  3. Hold steady until the scanner recognizes the code.
  4. Verify the scanned information in the form.
  5. Adjust details if necessary and submit.
